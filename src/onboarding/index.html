What is Ephemeral?

Ephemeral gives you temporary containers – isolated spaces for browsing that don't leave traces.

Think of it like a private browsing tab, but even more isolated and automatic. When you close it, everything inside is erased.

🧹
Complete cleanup

Cookies, site storage, cache, everything for that container – gone.

⌨️

How to open a private tab

The fastest way is a hotkey – no mouse needed.

Ctrl + Shift + E New ephemeral tab (auto-cleans on close)
Ctrl + Shift + U New ephemeral space (stays open for many tabs)

You can change these in Firefox Add-ons → Gear → Manage Shortcuts

🖱️
Or use your mouse

Click the Ephemeral toolbar button, or right-click any link → Open in ephemeral tab

🔒

How it stays private

Each ephemeral tab lives in its own isolated container.

🍪 Cookies ✓
💾 Site storage ✓
🗄️ Cache ✓
👻 Service workers ✓
📑 Tabs & identity ✓

Firefox doesn't allow us to delete global history or saved passwords – we never touch those. We only clean what belongs to the ephemeral container.

🚀

You're ready!

That's it. No complicated settings needed – it just works.

When you close the last tab of an ephemeral container, it's automatically cleaned. You can also clean manually from the popup or dashboard.

Tip: The extension is invisible in background – no telemetry, no network, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
